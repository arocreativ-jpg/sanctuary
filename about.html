Process

01 DESIGN

Every piece starts as a vector. Around a hundred designs in the library, drawn over a decade.

02 LASER

Cut and engraved. The depth and the detail happen here.

03 INLAY

Pieces set into the surface, glued up, locked together — parquetry.

04 FINISH

Sanded smooth, then coated. Soft, smooth and long-lasting — a microscopic layer that fills the grain and holds up for years.

05 SIGN

Hand signed. Numbered if it's a limited edition. Maker’s mark engraved on the back.

[ A little loop of reuse ]

Everything is sourced locally — regionally, within a two-and-a-half-hour radius. Whether it's a restaurant table or a piece of hardwood for coasters.

The wood for the coasters and all the smaller products is reclaimed from the bigger ones. We don't go buy a piece of maple to turn into coasters — it's always offcuts or scrap from a shelf job or a table job. It's part of a little loop of reuse. Even the curved triangle cut out of a round coaster becomes a pendant inlay, or an earring. There's a little fractal of reuses.

Crystals are sourced locally as well — cut and cabochoned by local people.

"That word has lost a lot of meaning over the years, but there's no reason not to use it. Now is the time more than ever to redefine it. Sustainable here means it's regionally purchased, and it always has a second use."
