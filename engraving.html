The Promise — First-person moment 1

"What you want, where you want it."

When I'm shopping for something engraved, there's never any interface to tell the maker where the thing goes. So I have to message them and say — I care about where this sits, what font you're using, how big it is. I'm not going to assume you'll get it right. We'll work with you to put exactly what you want exactly where you want it on the product. And if something looks off, I deal with it personally.

How it works — 5 steps

01

Send the file. Highest-resolution photo, or highest-resolution file of your design — JPEG, PNG or AI. Those are the three we accept.

02

Get a proof. We send a preview before anything is cut.

03

Approve it. Changes are free at this stage. That's what the proof is for.

04

Deposit. The laser fires as soon as the deposit lands.

05

Ships in 7-15 days. In recycled packaging.

Add-on · File prep

Got a napkin drawing?

If your file isn't ready to cut, we'll format it. Most files take under thirty minutes and it's a flat fee — no design retainer, no hourly. If you've got a sketch, we can turn it into a sticker.

Ship us your product

Already have the product?

Ship it to us and we'll engrave it. People send everything from grinders to military insignia. You cover shipping in; engraving and return shipping are quoted as one flat rate.
