Policies

Shipping, Returns, Care

Sub Sanctuary

Shipping

Everything ships in recycled packaging. We try hard not to buy new boxes — so your order might turn up in a re-used mailer with the old label crossed out. That's on purpose. There'll be a note in the box explaining it.

In-stock items ship in 2–3 days. Made-to-order and custom work ships in 7–15 days.

Returns

30 days. If something's wrong, we want to know. Return shipping is covered by the sender, and we assess the piece once it's back in our hands rather than from a photograph.

Defects. Every piece is picked free of defects before it goes out. Stocked items like grinders should arrive untouched — if you find a scratch or a defect, we replace it.

Wood grain isn't a defect. Wood is a natural product with natural character. We won't accept a return for grain you don't like — but talk to us and we'll look at a replacement at a reduced cost.

Either way. If you're unhappy with your piece, we want to make it right. Whatever needs to happen, we'll make it happen.

Care

Coasters and surfaces are finished with the same coating used on restaurant tabletops — they'll take years of use and won't watermark. Wall art arrives ready to hang. Wallets take a damp cloth and nothing else.
