Jewelry

This is the richest part of the whole process — hardwood and crystal, laser engraved, inlaid by hand. Wire-wrapped silver work has priced most people out of owning anything like it. Wood is cheaper and laser work is faster, so we can make something genuinely beautiful for less.

Pendants are made to order. A baseline piece is two crystals, two inlays and a silver-plated chain. From there it's a question of wood, stone, depth, detail and chain. Roughly $250 at the base, up to around $500.
